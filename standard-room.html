Room Overview

A comfortable and elegantly appointed sanctuary offering cozy queen bedding, dedicated executive work space, high-speed Wi-Fi, individual air conditioning, and a modern en-suite bathroom.

Thoughtfully curated for business professionals, solo travelers, and couples visiting Gorakhpur, the Standard Room at Hotel Amara pairs peaceful tranquility with boutique hospitality. Enjoy restful nights on our orthopedic queen-size mattress dressed in crisp premium linens, catch up on work at your private executive desk, and stay connected with complimentary high-speed optical Wi-Fi.

Every detail is engineered for convenience — from individual climate control and 43" Smart LED TV to tea and coffee makers and 24/7 in-room dining direct from Amara Restaurant.
