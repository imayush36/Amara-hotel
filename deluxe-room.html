Room Overview

Immerse yourself in elevated comfort with our most popular Delux Room, equipped with plush bedding, dedicated work desk, smart LED TV, tea/coffee maker, and modern en-suite bathroom.

The Delux Room represents the quintessential Hotel Amara experience. Designed with a seamless blend of contemporary elegance and warm hospitality, this room features expansive living space, ambient reading lighting, a dedicated executive desk, and an en-suite bathroom designed with bespoke fittings.

Whether you are in Gorakhpur for an important corporate meeting or exploring the city with family, the Delux Room offers 24/7 in-room dining from Amara Restaurant, high-speed Wi-Fi, and personalized front desk concierge care around the clock.
