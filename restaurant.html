Restaurant Overview

Immerse yourself in elevated culinary luxury at Amara Restaurant, Gorakhpur's premier boutique dining destination inside Hotel Amara. Serving authentic Awadhi recipes, rich North Indian curries, Chinese delicacies, and breakfast buffets.

Amara Restaurant represents the quintessential culinary hallmark of Hotel Amara. Designed with a seamless blend of contemporary elegance, grand lighting, comfortable seating, and ambient background music, our dining hall offers an extraordinary setting for family gatherings, business lunches, and joyful celebrations.

Every dish is curated by our seasoned master chefs using hand-pounded spices, cold-pressed oils, fresh dairy, and premium ingredients. Backed by 24/7 dedicated room service for in-house hotel guests and warm concierge care, we ensure every meal is an unforgettable experience.
