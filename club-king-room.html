Room Overview

Our prestigious Club King Bed room offers generous living space, plush grand king mattress, executive seating salon, 55" 4K Smart TV, and VIP personalized care.

The flagship stay of Hotel Amara Gorakhpur. Designed for business leaders, honeymooners, and guests seeking unparalleled hospitality, the Club King Bed suite spans 460 square feet of curated luxury. Recline on an opulent Grand King-size orthopedic bed, entertain associates in the independent velvet seating salon, or immerse yourself in the deep soaking bathtub in your designer en-suite bathroom.

Guests of the Club King category receive VIP concierge attention, priority table reservations at Amara Restaurant, complimentary chef breakfast buffet, and flexible check-in assistance.
