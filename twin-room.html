Room Overview

Ideal for colleagues, friends, or traveling pairs. Features two individual single beds with ergonomic mattresses, dedicated work space, high-speed Wi-Fi, and complete comfort amenities.

Whether you are attending a corporate convention in Gorakhpur or traveling on a pilgrimage tour with a friend, our Twin Room is tailored for your practical independence and shared comfort. Two standalone single beds are paired with individualized reading sconces and charging hubs, ensuring complete personal privacy within a spacious 310 sq. ft. floor plan.

Complete with a dedicated business work station, 43" Smart LED TV, electric kettle with refreshments, and 24/7 in-room dining from Amara Restaurant.
